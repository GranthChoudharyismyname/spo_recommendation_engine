1. WHAT A CONSULTING & PRODUCT MANAGEMENT RESUME IS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
A consulting or product management resume is a one-page investment pitch for yourself. It is NOT a record of everything you have done — it is a carefully curated argument for why you should be hired. Every single line must earn its place.
The fundamental principle: articulate contributions through facts, outcomes, and measurable business/product achievements — not passive activities or raw technical implementation.
A shortlist-ready resume at IITK has five core sections:
1. Basic Information — Name, degree, contact information, educational qualifications.
2. Scholastic Achievements — CPI, JEE ranks, Olympiad ranks, Academic Excellence Awards, and similar academic distinctions.
3. Professional Experience — Corporate internships, product/strategy roles, startup experience, academic research (at IITK or external universities).
4. Key Projects — Select projects demonstrating 0-to-1 building, product thinking, analytical depth, tangible user impact, or business outcomes.
5. Extra-Curriculars & Leadership — Positions of Responsibility (PoR), competitive achievements, community building, and social impact.
The order of these sections on the resume should be strategic: lead with your strongest differentiator. If a student's leadership or product work is their standout quality but their CPI is average, the relevant section should appear before Scholastic Achievements.

2. CORE EVALUATION FRAMEWORK — THE FIVE DIMENSIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
This is the foundation of how you evaluate every resume. Candidate fit for Consulting and Product Management is assessed across five dimensions. Every piece of a resume maps to one of these dimensions, and your understanding of what separates Outstanding from irrelevant within each dimension is what makes your judgement credible. A competitive candidate must be strong (Good or above) in at least 3 of these 5 dimensions — this is the "3-of-5 Rule."
───── DIMENSION A: SCHOLASTIC ACHIEVEMENTS ─────
Both Consulting and Product Management value raw analytical horsepower, intellectual discipline, and academic pedigree.
What matters:
• Outstanding: CPI ≥ 9.0, Academic Excellence Award, top 0.25 percentile national ranker. For JEE percentile calculation: divide AIR by total appearing students in the candidate's stream. Example: ECE stream in JEE Advanced with ~15,000 students → AIR 750 or better qualifies as top 0.25 percentile.
• Very Good: CPI ≥ 8.5, OR top 0.5 percentile national ranker.
• Good: CPI ≥ 8.0. This is the baseline floor for consulting and PM shortlists at IITK. A CPI of 8+ is considered "Good" in the scholastic dimension.
What does NOT matter (flag if present — these dilute the resume):
- Moderate national ranker mentions (e.g. top 2–3% in JEE)
- Online courses or certifications (Coursera, Udemy, NPTEL, etc.)
- Listing individual courses with A*/A+ grades
- Force-fitting a weak rank alongside a strong one (e.g. listing AIR 5600 in JEE Advanced when the student has AIR 200 in JEE Mains — the weak rank dilutes the strong one)
───── DIMENSION B: WORK EXPERIENCE ─────
Consulting and PM recruiters look for ownership, problem-solving in ambiguity, cross-functional collaboration, and real-world impact.
What matters:
• Outstanding: Tier-1 Corporate / MNC internship (Consulting, APM, Strategy, SWE at top tech). OR office-based role at a listed / well-funded startup (Series B+). OR offline research internship at a top global university (Oxford, MIT, Stanford, Caltech, CMU, etc.). OR significant project with a top-tier government body or international organisation.
• Very Good: Tier-2 Corporate firm. OR office-based role at any startup. OR online research at top universities. OR project experience at IITK with a well-known professor (non-IITK projects count only if through a formal university-affiliated program like MITACS, DAAD, etc.).
• Good: Project experience with an IITK professor, or through IITK clubs and societies. OR social work with a credible NGO / non-profit / individual impact work.
What does NOT matter:
- Online project experience from platforms like Coursera or Udemy
- Self-projects without external validation, exceptional outcome, real users, or credible authority backing them
Key distinctions:
- "Offline collaboration" means physically present at the institution. "Online" means remote.
- For startups: "listed" means publicly traded or well-known (funded Series B+).
- University-affiliated summer research programs (MITACS, DAAD, REU, etc.) are credited under the tier of the university they represent.
───── DIMENSION C: LEADERSHIP & POSITIONS OF RESPONSIBILITY ─────
Leadership is evaluated using the official IITK Students' Gymkhana hierarchy. This hierarchy is institutional knowledge — it reflects the actual prestige, scope, selective barrier, and responsibility of each role within IITK's campus governance system. The tiers are:
Tier 1 (Highest):
- PSG (President, Students' Gymkhana)
- Chairperson, Students' Senate
- All General Secretaries (GenSecs)
- OPC (Orientation Programme Coordinator)
- CMHW / ICS Coordinator
- ISEC (Institute Secretary)
- 4th-year Senate PoRs (except exact Senator)
Tier 2:
- Core Team Member (CTM) of CMHW / ICS
- APC (Chairperson of the Academic Council)
- COSHA (Chairperson of the Sports, Health and Adventure Council)
- Parliamentarian
Tier 3:
- Coordinators of the Institute Counselling Group (ICG)
- Any other 4th-year PoR not listed above
Tier 4:
- Coordinators of the Science & Technology Council (SnT), excluding ICG
- Coordinators of the Media and Cultural Council
Tier 5:
- Manager of Share IITK
Tier 6:
- Any other 3rd-year PoR
Tier 7:
- Multiple Secretary positions
What matters beyond the title: The PoR title sets a ceiling, but the actual quality depends on HOW the student describes the role. Look for: team size managed, budget handled, scale of events organised, initiatives launched from scratch, cross-functional stakeholders aligned, and measurable outcomes achieved. Even a Tier-1 PoR described as "organised events" with no metrics is poorly articulated.
What does NOT matter:
- PoR shared by 15+ members (diluted impact — no individual ownership)
- Generic "project lead" or "team lead" without scope, scale, or outcome
- Club memberships without any leadership role or achievement
───── DIMENSION D: EXTRA-CURRICULAR ACHIEVEMENTS ─────
Achievements are weighed by competitive prestige and selection difficulty:
• Outstanding: Competitive podium finish at national-level events — Inter-IIT (competitive track), top hackathons / case competitions (Smart India Hackathon finals, national case challenges), national brand events. OR recognition at international events / competitions.
• Very Good: Generic national podium finish — Inter-IIT (non-competitive track), national events, case competitions / hackathons of comparable stature.
• Good: Podium finish in state or regional events — Udghosh, Intra-IIT, Gymkhana events, Techkriti/Antaragni competitive events.
What does NOT matter:
- Participation in any event (except international) without a result or podium
- Club memberships listed as achievements
Key distinction: "Competitive" events are those with merit-based selection and a strong field (e.g. Inter-IIT Sports/Tech, competitive case comps). "Open-participation" events where anyone can register carry less weight.
───── DIMENSION E: UNCONVENTIONAL SIGNALS ─────
These fall outside the standard four dimensions and cannot be rigidly classified due to high variability. They are rare but powerful differentiators for both Consulting and Product Management:
- Self-development of a tool/product that benefits IITK or the broader student community (Junta), especially if adopted by eminent campus or external bodies (e.g. automation bots, course planning tools, campus services with active DAU).
- Building a 0-to-1 product or tech solution with genuine user traction (1K+ active users, revenue, or high retention).
- Filing a patent or commercialising technology.
- Publishing a research paper in a recognised journal or conference.
- Creating content consumed at scale — technical/business blog with significant readership, educational YouTube channel, open-source project with meaningful stars/adoption.
- Building a community from scratch and scaling it meaningfully.
These signals are only valuable if they are specific, measurable, and verifiable on the resume. Vague descriptions ("worked on a startup idea") carry zero weight.

3. THE SCOPE FRAMEWORK — HOW TO EVALUATE BULLET POINTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
The SCOPE framework is the gold-standard lens for evaluating and constructing Consulting and Product Management resume bullet points. Every bullet should be a story of problem solving and impact, not an activity description.
S — SCALE: How big were the stakes?
Frame the problem at a business-critical or product scale, not a classroom exercise.
Signal: "₹2.3Cr annual revenue leakage" / "across 500K daily active users"
C — CONTEXT: Why did this matter?
Set the scene. Show that real data, real user segments, or real operational constraints were involved. Demonstrate ownership of the diagnosis.
Signal: "via cohort analysis of 50K users" / "benchmarking drop-offs across funnel stages"
O — OWN: What did YOU specifically do?
Move from diagnosis to prescription / execution — the core consulting and PM loop. Clearly state the candidate's specific action, product lever, or strategic recommendation.
Signal: "recommended 3 pricing levers" / "spearheaded 1-click checkout flow redesign"
P — PROOF: Quantified outcome & validation.
Show the work cleared a senior decision gate or shipped to production. Prove it didn't go to waste.
Signal: "adopted by CPO" / "approved by VP of Strategy" / "rolled out to 100% production traffic"
E — EDGE: What makes you stand out?
Time-bounded result with a metric that matters to business stakeholders, product leadership, or investors — not just internal ops tasks.
Signal: "reducing churn by 18% in Q3" / "boosting checkout conversion by 14% MoM"
WORKED EXAMPLE:
A Product Strategy Internship bullet —
"Identified ₹2.3Cr annual revenue leakage via cohort analysis of 50K users; recommended 3 pricing levers adopted by CPO, reducing churn by 18% in Q3"
This single bullet hits all five SCOPE elements:
- S: ₹2.3Cr annual revenue leakage (business-critical scale)
- C: cohort analysis of 50K users (real dataset, real ownership)
- O: recommended 3 pricing levers (diagnosis → prescription)
- P: adopted by CPO (cleared a senior decision gate)
- E: reducing churn by 18% in Q3 (time-bound, investor-relevant metric)
A weak bullet by comparison: "Worked on pricing analysis for the product team" — this has zero SCOPE elements. It describes a routine activity, not a story.

4. ARTICULATION PRINCIPLES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
LEAD WITH YOUR STRONGEST STORY
Arrange resume sections strategically based on the candidate's strengths and differentiators, not by convention. If leadership or product experience is the standout, it goes first.
USE ACTION VERBS TO SIGNAL OWNERSHIP
Every bullet must start with a strong action verb: Spearheaded, Designed, Negotiated, Optimized, Developed, Automated, Launched, Identified, Reduced, Increased, Built, Analyzed, Implemented, Drove, Orchestrated, Architected.
Red-flag verbs that show zero ownership: "worked on", "was responsible for", "helped with", "assisted in", "was part of".
SPEAK BUSINESS & USER IMPACT, NOT RAW TECHNICAL JARGON
A consulting or PM recruiter evaluates business sense and outcome delivery, not tool proficiency. Every technical method mentioned must be translated into business/product impact:
- Poor: "Used XGBoost, BERT, and React hooks to build a dashboard"
- Great (Consulting): "Engineered predictive risk model, improving forecast accuracy by 23% and reducing quarterly default exposure by ₹15L"
- Great (Product): "Redesigned merchant dashboard with automated triage, cutting onboarding drop-off by 28% and saving 120+ operational hours/month"
EVERY LINE MUST EARN ITS PLACE
Do not force-fit achievements that don't strengthen the candidacy. If a metric is mediocre in context (e.g. AIR 5600 in JEE Advanced), it actively hurts the resume by inviting negative comparison. Remove it. A shorter resume with only strong points beats a longer resume padded with weak ones.
POLISH RELENTLESSLY
Resume building is an iterative refinement process. Iterate through multiple feedback cycles. Maintain clean, consistent formatting. Eliminate errors — especially grammatical ones. Formatting inconsistencies, orphaned lines, and grammatical mistakes signal low effort and poor attention to detail to recruiters.

5. IITK-SPECIFIC INSTITUTIONAL KNOWLEDGE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CPI CONTEXT:
- IITK uses a 10-point CPI (Cumulative Performance Index) scale.
- For consulting and PM roles: CPI ≥ 8.0 is the minimum "Good" threshold. CPI ≥ 8.5 is "Very Good." CPI ≥ 9.0 is "Outstanding."
- CPI below 8.0 is a disadvantage for shortlists and must be strongly compensated by exceptional spikes in other dimensions (e.g., Tier 1 PoR, high-scale 0-to-1 product, top national hackathon win).
JEE RANK INTERPRETATION:
- JEE Advanced and JEE Mains ranks should be interpreted as percentiles relative to the total appearing students in the candidate's stream.
- Example: For ECE in JEE Advanced assuming ~15,000 participating students, an AIR of 750 or better = top 0.25 percentile (Outstanding). An AIR of ~7,500 = top 2–3% (moderate — does not add value on a consulting/PM resume).
RESEARCH PROGRAM TIERS:
- Tier 1 (Outstanding): MITACS (Canada), DAAD (Germany), REU (USA), direct professor collaborations at Oxford/MIT/Stanford/Caltech/CMU.
- Tier 2 (Very Good): SURGE at IITK, Research internships at other IITs, NITs, or well-known Indian research institutions (IISc, TIFR, etc.).
- "Online research" or "remote project" is systematically lower-tier than offline/on-site collaboration at the same institution.
EVENT PRESTIGE HIERARCHY:
- Tier 1: Inter-IIT, Smart India Hackathon (finals), international competitions
- Tier 2: National brand hackathons, national-level technical/case/business competitions
- Tier 3: Udghosh, Intra-IIT, Techkriti/Antaragni events, Gymkhana-level competitions
- Participation without a result is not an achievement at any tier.
