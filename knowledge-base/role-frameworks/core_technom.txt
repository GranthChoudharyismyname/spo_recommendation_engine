1. WHAT A CORE & TECHNOM RESUME IS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
A Core and Techno-Managerial resume must strike a balance between hard technical foundation (domain engineering concepts, hands-on professor-led projects) and operational leadership (managing teams, on-ground execution, crisis management, physical resilience).
Recruiters for TechnoM roles (e.g. SLB Field Engineer Trainee, HUL Supply Chain ULIP, Nestlé Operations, ITC Technical Management) look for:
1. Technical Competence: Solid foundational engineering understanding and professor-backed research/projects.
2. High Ownership & Ground Leadership: Demonstrated ability to manage crews, coordinate logistics, and lead large teams under high-pressure, physical/operational environments.
3. Problem Solving on the Shop Floor / Field: Hands-on debugging, process optimization, safety compliance, throughput improvement, and resource efficiency.

2. CORE EVALUATION BENCHMARKS — THE SIX PILLARS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
───── PILLAR 1: BRANCH ELIGIBILITY & HIERARCHY ─────
Core and TechnoM recruitment at IITK strictly focuses on core engineering disciplines:
Eligible Branches: Electrical Engineering (EE), Mechanical Engineering (ME), Chemical Engineering (CHE), and Materials Science & Engineering (MSE).
Branch Hierarchy & Preference:
• EE (Electrical Engineering): Highest Preference / Tier 1 (Universal demand across hardware, automation, power, instrumentation, and techno-management).
• ME (Mechanical Engineering): High Preference / Tier 2 (Primary target for field engineering, automotive, heavy machinery, thermodynamics, and manufacturing plants).
• CHE (Chemical Engineering): Strong Preference / Tier 3 (Core demand in oil & gas, FMCG supply chain, petrochemicals, process engineering, and refineries).
• MSE (Materials Science & Engineering): Eligible / Tier 4 (Valued in metallurgy, materials characterization, failure analysis, and quality control).
• Other Branches: Non-core branches are generally filtered out unless the company explicitly opens for all departments.
───── PILLAR 2: CPI (ACADEMIC PERFORMANCE) ─────
Core companies use CPI as a strong indicator of technical consistency and academic discipline.
• CPI ≥ 8.5: Outstanding (Guaranteed academic clearance across all top-tier Core & TechnoM MNCs; strong differentiator for R&D and leadership tracks).
• CPI 8.0 – 8.5: Very Good (Comfortably above shortlisting cutoffs for almost all firms).
• CPI 7.5 – 8.0: Good (Solid standing; meets criteria for SLB, HUL, Nestlé, ITC, Shell).
• CPI 6.5 – 7.5: Average / Baseline (Meets minimum eligibility for general core roles, but requires strong PoRs or core projects to stand out).
• CPI < 6.5: Below Minimum / High Risk (Most core companies enforce a strict 6.5 or 7.0 hard cutoff; significant risk of elimination at resume drop).
───── PILLAR 3: CORE PROJECTS (PROFESSOR-GUIDED) ─────
Having at least ONE substantial branch-related core project is a good-to-have plus point.
• Critical Rule: ONLY Professor-Guided Projects are counted as formal core projects (e.g. BTP - Bachelor Thesis Project, UGP - Undergraduate Project, SURGE at IITK, or research internships with faculty from IITs/top global universities).
• Valid Substitute: If a formal professor-guided project is absent, having served as a **Project Mentor** (e.g. mentoring junior batches in technical clubs, SnT Council summer projects, or departmental technical initiatives) serves as a strong substitute that demonstrates both technical depth and mentorship/leadership.
• Independent self-projects, casual hobby builds, or unguided online tutorial tasks carry negligible weight for core engineering evaluation.
• Strong Project Signals: Experimental test rigs, numerical simulations (ANSYS, COMSOL, SolidWorks, Aspen Plus, MATLAB/Simulink, Cadence), hardware prototyping, finite element analysis (FEA), computational fluid dynamics (CFD), PLC/SCADA automation, material synthesis/characterization (XRD, SEM, TEM).
───── PILLAR 4: RELEVANT COURSEWORK (MANDATORY TO HIGHLIGHT) ─────
Core recruiters look directly at the coursework section to ensure the candidate has studied foundational subjects relevant to operations, manufacturing, thermal systems, and plant engineering.
Required / Branch-Specific Key Courses:
- Mechanical / Chemical / Materials: Thermodynamics, Fluid Mechanics, Heat & Mass Transfer, Manufacturing Processes & Production Technology, Mechanics of Solids, Chemical Reaction Engineering, Material Characterization, Phase Transformations.
- Electrical: Circuit Theory, Power Systems, Power Electronics, Control Systems, Signals & Systems, Microprocessors & Embedded Systems.
Feedback Rule: If a candidate has NOT included a "Relevant Coursework" section, or has omitted fundamental courses remotely related to Thermodynamics, Manufacturing, Fluid Mechanics, or Control Systems, you MUST explicitly recommend adding them.
───── PILLAR 5: LEADERSHIP & POSITIONS OF RESPONSIBILITY (PoR) ─────
Leadership is extraordinarily valued in Techno-Managerial and Field roles because candidates will directly manage technicians, ground workers, contractors, and cross-functional plant teams.
Evaluate PoR using the recognized IITK Gymkhana Hierarchy:
Tier 1 (Highest):
- PSG (President, Students' Gymkhana)
- Chairperson, Students' Senate
- All General Secretaries (GenSecs)
- OPC (Orientation Programme Coordinator)
- CMHW / ICS Coordinator
- ISEC (Institute Secretary)
- 4th-year Senate PoRs (except exact Senator)
Tier 2:
- Core Team Member (CTM) of CMHW / ICS
- APC (Chairperson of the Academic Council)
- COSHA (Chairperson of the Sports, Health and Adventure Council)
- Parliamentarian
Tier 3:
- Coordinators of the Institute Counselling Group (ICG)
- Any other 4th-year PoR not listed above
Tier 4:
- Coordinators of the Science & Technology Council (SnT), excluding ICG
- Coordinators of the Media and Cultural Council
Tier 5:
- Manager of Share IITK
Tier 6:
- Any other 3rd-year PoR
Tier 7:
- Multiple Secretary positions
Beyond the title, look for:
- Team size managed (e.g., led 40+ members across 3 verticals).
- Budget & vendor management (e.g., managed ₹25L+ budget, negotiated with 15+ external vendors).
- Crisis handling and logistics (e.g., managed footfall of 20,000+ during fest, zero incident safety record).
───── PILLAR 6: WORK EXPERIENCE & RESEARCH PEDIGREE ─────
Core and TechnoM recruiters evaluate practical engineering competence through two tracks: Industry/Operations Experience and Academic/Industrial Research.
A. Industry & Corporate Work Experience:
• Outstanding:
  - On-site plant/refinery/manufacturing/field engineering internship at major industrial conglomerates (Tata Steel Jamshedpur, Reliance Jamnagar, SLB/Schlumberger, Shell, HUL Supply Chain/Plants, ITC, Bajaj Auto, Maruti Suzuki, Bosch, Texas Instruments, GE, Baker Hughes).
  - Operations / Supply Chain internship with demonstrable shop-floor ownership (e.g. throughput increase, cycle time reduction, inventory optimization, equipment reliability).
• Very Good:
  - Technical engineering or operations internship at mid-scale manufacturing firms, automotive tier-1 suppliers, or industrial/hardware startups.
• Good:
  - General operations, logistics, or engineering trainee experience.
• Not Important / Diluting:
  - Generic frontend/web dev CRUD apps, basic data entry, or non-technical marketing/sales roles (signals lack of interest in core).
B. Academic & Industrial Research Experience:
• Outstanding:
  - Offline experimental or computational engineering research internship at top global universities (MIT, Stanford, Oxford, Cambridge, Imperial College, Purdue, RWTH Aachen, TU Munich, NTU, NUS).
  - Prestigious international research fellowships: DAAD WISE (Germany), MITACS Globalink (Canada), SN Bose Fellowship, Charpak (France).
  - First-author or co-author paper published in reputed engineering journals or conferences (ASME, IEEE Transactions, Elsevier, Springer, AIChE).
• Very Good:
  - SURGE at IITK in core experimental or simulation engineering labs (Thermo-fluids, Combustion, Materials, Power Electronics, Robotics).
  - Summer research at premier Indian research institutions: IISc Bangalore, IITB, IITD, IITM, BARC, ISRO, DRDO, CSIR labs.
• Good:
  - Formal BTP (Bachelor Thesis Project) or UGP (Undergraduate Project) at IITK with core engineering faculty.
• Not Important / Diluting:
  - Informal/remote online projects without experimental validation, hardware fabrication, or formal professor recommendation.
